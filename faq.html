Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about BN Ration Scale.

Home  ›  FAQ

1. What is BN Ration Scale?

It is a web‑based calculator that helps compute ration quantities based on personnel strength, category (Officers, JCO/ORs, Army Dog), ration type (Fresh/Dry), and number of days.

2. Is it an official government or military tool?

No. BN Ration Scale is an independent project by StartKnowledge. It is not affiliated with the Indian Army, Ministry of Defence, or any government body. It is provided for informational and planning assistance only.

3. How accurate are the calculations?

We strive for accuracy, but we do not guarantee that the scales or outputs are error‑free or up‑to‑date. Always verify results against current official documents before making any decisions.

4. What categories are available?

Currently: Officers (OFFRS), JCO/ORs, and Army Dogs. Each category has separate Fresh and Dry ration scales.

5. Can I use it offline?

The website requires an internet connection to load. However, you can save the page locally for offline use, though some dynamic features may not work.

6. How do I report a bug or error?

Please email support@startknowledge.in with details of the issue. See our Support page for guidance.

7. Where can I find the Fresh Vegetable Variety Chart?

Visit the dedicated Veg Fresh Variety Chart page, where you can select a month to view itemised quantities.

8. Do you store my personal data?

We do not store any personal data unless you voluntarily contact us. See our Privacy Policy for details.

9. Are there affiliate links or ads on the site?

Yes, we display advertisements (e.g., Google AdSense) and may use affiliate links. These help support the hosting and development of the project. See our Additional Information and Disclaimer for more.

10. How can I suggest a new feature?

We welcome suggestions! Please email contact@startknowledge.in with your idea.

11. What are the system requirements?

Any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) with JavaScript enabled. The site is responsive and works on desktops, tablets, and mobile phones.

12. Can I use this for official planning?

No. This tool is for reference only. Official planning must follow the latest authorised orders and scales.

13. How often is the data updated?

Updates are made periodically when new data is available. Check the Changelog for version history.

14. Do you use AI or APIs?

The BN Ration Scale calculator itself does not use AI or external APIs. However, other StartKnowledge projects may. See the Additional page for more.
