Version history of BN Ration Scale – updates, fixes, and new features.

Version 1.0 – 2026-09-03

Initial Release

  • Core Calculator: Support for Officers, JCO/ORs, and Army Dog categories, with Fresh and Dry ration types.
  • Calculation Logic: Strength × Days × Per‑head scale.
  • Vegetable Variety Chart: Monthly selection with itemised fresh vegetable quantities.
  • Responsive Design: Works on all devices.
  • Informational Pages: About, Contact, Privacy, Cookies, Terms, Disclaimer, Support, FAQ, Documentation, Additional, Changelog.
  • SEO: Meta tags, Open Graph, Twitter Cards, JSON‑LD.
  • Advertising & Affiliate: Sections added to relevant pages.
  • Legal Pages: Comprehensive disclaimers and policies.

Planned for v1.1

  • Add "Families" category.
  • Export results to PDF.
  • Option to save custom scales.
  • Improved mobile navigation.
  • More detailed error handling.

Bug Fixes (v1.0.1 – future)

  • Fixed rounding issues in decimal quantities.
  • Corrected vegetable percentages for certain months.
