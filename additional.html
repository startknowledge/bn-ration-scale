Project Background

BN Ration Scale was conceived as a practical utility to assist users in calculating ration requirements for a battalion‑like setup. It is part of the StartKnowledge ecosystem, which includes a range of calculators, technical tools, and educational content. The project emphasises simplicity, transparency, and accessibility.

The calculator is built with standard web technologies and does not rely on server‑side processing, making it easy to host and maintain. All calculations are performed client‑side, ensuring that your data does not leave your device.
