Response Times

We aim to respond to all inquiries in a timely manner. The table below provides an indication of expected response times based on the nature of your inquiry.

  • General Questions & Feedback: Usually within 48–72 hours.
  • Technical Issues & Bug Reports: Usually within 24–48 hours for initial acknowledgment.
  • Feature Requests & Suggestions: Usually within 72 hours; we may follow up for clarification.
  • Support & Help Requests: Usually within 24–48 hours depending on complexity.
  • Partnership & Collaboration: Usually within 3–5 working days.

During holidays, weekends or high-volume periods, response times may be longer. We appreciate your patience and understanding.

Before You Contact Us

To help us assist you more efficiently, please consider the following suggestions before reaching out:

  • Check the FAQ: Many common questions are already answered in the Frequently Asked Questions page.
  • Review Documentation: The Documentation page contains technical information about the calculator's structure, formulas and implementation.
  • Read the How-to Guide: The How to Use page provides step-by-step instructions for operating the calculator.
  • Check the Disclaimer: The Disclaimer explains the limitations, responsibilities and intended use of the website.
  • Review the Changelog: The Changelog shows recent updates, fixes and improvements made to the project.

If you have already checked these resources and still need assistance, please use the contact form or email addresses above. Be sure to include relevant details such as the browser you are using, the steps you took, and any error messages you received.

Privacy & Data Handling

Your privacy is important to us. When you contact us through the contact form or via email, we use the information you provide solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

For more details about how we handle information, please read our Privacy Policy and Cookies Policy.

Secure Communication: While we take reasonable measures to protect the information you send to us, no email or web form is completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive, confidential or classified information through these channels.
